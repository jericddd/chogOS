# What to show

The older recording script in this file told a presenter to say that ChogBank and the citizen wallets were not deployed. That script is withdrawn. The demo that matches the live app is the one on the app itself.

Open [https://chogos.vercel.app/](https://chogos.vercel.app/).

1. World. Watch the map. Click a citizen. Click Commons Hall. Read a Town growth row and the reason under it.
2. Citizens. Open the same token. The portrait, token id, and joined date are that NFT. On a founding citizen the treasury address is the NFT holder, not the citizen wallet.
3. Economy. Read the highlighted MON and `$CHOG` balances, then the contract caps: 0.01 MON per transaction and 1 wei of `$CHOG`. A Confirmed wallet row has a Monad transaction. A decision row is not itself a transfer.
4. History. Source AI is a stored reason. Category Onchain is a Monad receipt.
5. Verify. The browser recomputes the hash. Then use Transaction, Citizen wallet, Wallet factory, and Genesis token.
6. Enroll. A holder connects a wallet that currently owns a Chog Genesis NFT and signs the confirmation. Judging the world does not require that step.
